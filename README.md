# 🏏 2025 IPL Data Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive and comprehensive end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing player and team performance metrics across the **IPL 2025 season**. The objective of this project is to evaluate bowling efficiency, batting contribution, dismissals distribution, and match outcomes through dynamic visualizations and DAX measures.

---

## 📊 Key KPIs & Metrics Tracked
- **Runs Conceded:** Total runs given (26K+)
- **Overs Bowled:** Cumulative bowling volume (2.74K overs)
- **Boundary Distribution:** Total Sixes (1K+) and Fours (2K+)
- **Dot Balls & Wickets:** 5K total dot balls bowled and 873 total wickets captured
- **Scoring Analysis:** Singles (6K) and Doubles (968) performance metrics

---

## 📈 Visualizations & Analytical Highlights
1. **Bowling Impact:** Bar chart detailing top individual wicket-takers across matches.
2. **Boundary Contribution:** Distribution of total sixes hit by individual player teams (PBKS, LSG, RR, etc.).
3. **Dismissal Patterns:** Horizontal breakdown of dismissal modes (Caught leading, Bowled, LBW, Run Out, Stumped).
4. **Scoring Share:** Donut chart showcasing the proportional distribution of runs scored across competing franchises.
5. **Batting Average Performance:** Treemap comparing average runs scored per team (GT, RCB, PBKS, SRH, etc.).
6. **Venue Mapping:** Interactive map visualization plotting matches across stadiums (Eden Gardens, MA Chidambaram, Barsapara Stadium, etc.).
7. **Team Bowling Benchmarks:** Stacked horizontal bar chart measuring top wickets taken per bowling unit.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
- **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling & DAX:** Custom calculated measures, KPI cards, aggregations, and ratios
- **Visuals Used:** Cards, Donut Chart, Treemap, Map Visual, Stacked Bar/Column Charts, Slicers
- **Interactivity:** Dynamic filtering via Date and Team slicers

---

## 🚀 How to View
1. Download the `.pbix` file from the repository.
2. Open with **Power BI Desktop** to interact with filters and slicers dynamically.


# 🍔 Swiggy Food Delivery Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing customer order patterns, delivery speeds, payment preferences, and cuisine ratings for **Swiggy**. The project focuses on assessing operational delivery metrics alongside revenue drivers across major city locations (Hyderabad, Secunderabad, etc.).

---

## 📊 Key KPIs & Metrics Tracked
- **Total Swiggy Revenue:** ₹ 26.64K
- **Total Orders:** 26 orders evaluated
- **Average Rating:** 3.88 / 5.0 overall customer satisfaction
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹ 1,025 per order

---

## 📈 Visualizations & Analytical Highlights
1. **Delivery Speed by Metrics & Performance:** Line and clustered column charts evaluating delivery turnaround times across different operational speeds (Speed Delivery, Medium Speed, Slow Delivery).
2. **Average Rating by Food Category:** Horizontal bar chart benchmarking ratings across food types (South Indian leading with 5.0, followed by Bakery & Desserts, Fast Food, North Indian, Chinese, and Biryani).
3. **Total Revenue by Food Category:** Bar chart highlighting total sales volume generated per cuisine type (Biryani leading revenue generation).
4. **Sales by Payment Method:** Donut/Pie chart breakdown of transaction modes showing customer preference distribution across Online, UPI, Card, and Cash.
5. **Geographical Order Mapping:** Interactive map visualization tracking delivery locations across Hyderabad and Secunderabad localities (Banjara Hills, Khairatabad, Balanagar, Marredpally, etc.).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
- **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling & DAX:** Custom calculated measures for Revenue, Average Ratings, and AOV
- **Visuals Used:** KPI Cards, Line & Clustered Column Charts, Bar Charts, Pie Chart, Map Visual, and Food Name Slicers
- **Data Slicing:** Category filtering by Bakery & Desserts, Biryani, Chinese, and Fast Food

---

## 🚀 How to View
1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open it with **Power BI Desktop** to explore the interactive slicers and drill-downs.
